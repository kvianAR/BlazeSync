# BlazeSync 0.4.1

- Open the official Backblaze billing portal from Storage & billing with Pay on Backblaze.
- Choose a monthly payment day from 1–31, with an option to disable reminders.
- Receive a daily system reminder from seven days before your chosen date through the payment date, after 9 AM local time.
- Preserve the reminder date and daily delivery marker across app restarts.
- Adjust days 29–31 to the last day of shorter months.
- Recheck reminders after sleep or unlock while BlazeSync is running.

Keep BlazeSync running in the menu bar and enable its macOS notifications for system reminders. Reminders follow your chosen date. Payment and invoice management happen in your Backblaze account.

Validated with 81 unit/regression tests and 52 packaged desktop checks against a synthetic S3 fixture. Reminder calendar and duplicate prevention are covered by automated tests. System notification delivery depends on macOS notification permissions and Focus settings.
